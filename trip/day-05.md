## Main activity 1 — Lago di Misurina

- **What:** Largest natural lake in Cadore (0.4 km²) at 1,754 m, nicknamed the "Pearl of Cadore"
- **Walk:** Easy flat perimeter, ~2.6 km, no elevation. Paved/gravel mix. ~45 min relaxed pace.
- **Why:** Reflections of the Sorapiss group to the south and the Tre Cime to the north. A good warm-up and loosening walk before the Tre Cime drive up.
- **Crowds:** Busy in the middle of the day — arriving ~10:00 is quiet.
- **Facilities:** Cafés, toilets, paid parking (~€3/hr).

## Main activity 2 — Tre Cime di Lavaredo viewpoint (Rifugio Auronzo → Rifugio Lavaredo)

- **What:** The most iconic image of the Dolomites — three vertical rock towers (Cima Grande 2,999 m in the middle).
- **Walk:** The graded, mostly-flat path from the **Rifugio Auronzo** parking area (2,333 m) to **Rifugio Lavaredo** (2,344 m) and back. ~4 km out-and-back, ~1h 30 if you walk slowly and stop for photos.
- **Why this version (short loop) and not the full 3-hour circuit:** The short version still gives the famous north-face view of the Three Peaks, and lets us leave by ~13:30 to be home by ~17:00. The full loop via Rifugio Locatelli adds ~2h and would blow the departure constraint.
- **Footwear:** Trail shoes are fine; no technical terrain.
- **Weather:** The plateau is exposed — bring a wind layer even in July/August. Morning is usually clearest; afternoon clouds often build up by 13:00.

## Tre Cime toll road — practical notes

- **Cost:** ~€30 per car, round trip, paid at the gate at Lago Antorno (between Misurina and Rifugio Auronzo). Cash and card both accepted.
- **Hours (July/August):** ~06:00–20:00. Cars must be off the road by the closing time.
- **Parking:** Rifugio Auronzo has a large paved lot. It **can fill by mid-morning** on sunny weekends — arriving ~11:00 is normally OK but not guaranteed.
- **Don't skip Misurina:** If Rifugio Auronzo is full, drop the Tre Cime drive and extend Misurina, or park at the overflow and shuttle (signed on-site).

## Practical notes

- **Fuel:** Fill up in Italy (Auronzo or Tolmezzo) before Tarvisio — fuel in Italy is usually cheaper than in Carinthia.
- **Bad weather plan:** If Tre Cime is clouded in, do **Misurina only** and depart by ~11:30. The iconic view is gone in fog, and the toll is €30 either way.
- **No cable car / chairlift this day** — everything is road + short walks.
- **Altitude at Rifugio Auronzo (2,333 m):** Can feel cool even in August, and UV is strong. Sunscreen + layer.
