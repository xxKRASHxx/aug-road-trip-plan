## Main activity — Cinque Torri

- **What:** Five iconic Dolomite rock towers (Cinque Torri = "Five Towers") rising from a high plateau at ~2,200 m. The surrounding area includes WWI trenches and fortifications preserved in-situ.
- **Walk:** Easy flat circuit around the towers, ~2 km loop, minimal elevation gain. **No hiking boots required** — trail shoes are fine.
- **Chairlift:** Seggiovia Cinque Torri from the Bai de Dones base station. Return ticket ~€15 / person. Operates from mid-June through September.
- **July–August:** Arrive early (before 10:00) to beat queues at the chairlift and have the plateau to yourself for photos
- **WWI trenches:** Well-preserved open-air museum with informational plaques — add 30 min if interested
- **Weather:** The plateau is exposed — bring a wind layer even in summer

## Practical notes

- **Cinque Torri chairlift hours:** Typically 09:00–17:00 in July–August; check https://www.dolomiti.org closer to the date
- **Chairlift queue:** Peak days (weekend, sunny) can have 20–30 min waits. Arriving at 09:15–09:30 usually avoids the main rush
- **No hiking boots needed:** The plateau loop is a well-maintained gravel path. Trail shoes or sneakers are sufficient
- **Alternative if time or weather is bad:** Drive up to **Passo Falzarego** (5 min from chairlift base) — dramatic rock wall panorama from the car park, no walking required. Then depart earlier
- **Fuel:** Fill up in Italy (generally cheaper than Austria) before crossing back at Tarvisio
