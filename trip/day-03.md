## ⚠️ Weather check — mandatory before starting

Check **both** of these the morning of Day 3:

- **MeteoAustria / Tirol:** https://www.zamg.ac.at/cms/de/wetter/wetter-oesterreich/tirol

## Main activity — Stuibenfall Klettersteig

- **What:** Via ferrata (Klettersteig) alongside the Stuibenfall, Austria's highest waterfall (159 m). The route climbs iron rungs and cables bolted directly to the rock beside the cascade.
- **Access:** Entirely on foot from the Umhausen gear shop — you park once, walk ~15–30 min to the ferrata base, and walk back the same way. **No separate drive out to a trailhead.**
- **Difficulty:** B/C (moderate–difficult on the UIAA via ferrata scale). Exposure is significant — sections over the waterfall gorge with substantial drop below.
- **Duration:** Approach 20–30 min + ferrata 2.5–3h + descent 45 min = ~4–4.5h door to door
- **No own gear:** See rental and guide options below
- **Footwear:** Approach shoes or stiff hiking boots — not trail runners
- **Helmet:** Mandatory (falling rock, swinging lanyard)

### Gear without own equipment

| Option | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Guide (recommended)** | A certified mountain guide provides full equipment + technique briefing + weather call. Book via Ötztal Mountain Guides 4–6 weeks ahead for July–August. Group rate often ~€60–120/person. |
| **Self + rental** | Only if at least one person has prior B/C ferrata experience. Rent Y-lanyard set + harness + helmet at a local shop. Get a full technique briefing from the shop. |

**Rental shops (Ötztal):**
- Search: `Sportgeschäft Umhausen` or `Skiverleih Imst Klettersteig`
- Or ask your accommodation host — local guesthouses often know the best options

## Practical notes

- **Parking:** Park once in Umhausen at / near the gear shop (coords `47.1108, 10.9241`). Everything else is walkable — no drive-and-return detour to a separate trailhead.
- **Day 3 driving is light (~1h15 total):** Fulpmes → Umhausen in the morning, Umhausen → Längenfeld at the end of the day. The entire middle of the day goes to the ferrata on foot. Do not rush the descent; fatigue accidents happen on the way down.
- **Day swap contingency:** If weather is bad on Day 3, drive to Cortina (Day 4's route) instead, and attempt ferrata on Day 4 morning if the forecast is better. Both days are structurally flexible.
