## Main activity — Nordkette

- **What:** Urban cable car system connecting Innsbruck city center to the alpine ridge above
- **Route:** Congress (631 m) → Hungerburg (860 m, funicular) → Seegrube (1,905 m, gondola) → Hafelekar (2,256 m, gondola)
- **Hike:** Hafelekar → Seegrube on the panorama trail, ~1h, moderate. Some sections are steep and rocky — wear proper footwear (not sandals)
- **Alternative (easier):** Skip Hafelekar gondola, do the easy plateau walk at Seegrube level (~45 min flat loop)
- **July–August:** Queues at the Hungerburg funicular and Seegrube gondola can be 20–30 min. **Book tickets online in advance.**
- **Ticket:** Full funicular + gondola (Hungerburg → Seegrube → Hafelekar) return: ~€40–45 / person (2024)

## Practical notes

- **Parking Innsbruck:** Use the Congressgarage (Rennweg 3) — closest to the funicular lower station. ~€3–4/hr. Alternatively, park at the Hungerburg P+R if driving up
- **Nordkette weather:** Check the [Nordkette webcam](https://www.nordkette.com/en/webcams) the morning of Day 2. Afternoon thunderstorms are possible in July–August — go up early in the day if forecast is uncertain
- **Innsbruck old town:** The Goldenes Dachl (Golden Roof) is the must-see; the courtyard and surrounding medieval streets are free to walk. Maria-Theresien-Straße has the best view of the Nordkette ridge from the city
