## Via ferrata kit (Day 3 — Stuibenfall)

### If using a guide (recommended)
The guide provides all technical equipment. Bring:
- [ ] Sturdy hiking boots (not trail runners — ankle support needed)
- [ ] Long trousers or hiking tights (rock abrasion)
- [ ] Windproof jacket (exposed sections beside waterfall are cold and wet)
- [ ] Gloves (optional but useful — iron rungs can be cold/wet)
- [ ] Water — minimum 1.5 L per person
- [ ] Snacks / lunch (no café on the route)
- [ ] Sunscreen + sunglasses (high altitude exposure)

### If renting gear independently
All of the above, plus confirm the rental shop provides:
- [ ] Harness (sized to fit — try on before leaving the shop)
- [ ] Via ferrata Y-lanyard (energy-absorbing, not a plain sling)
- [ ] Helmet
- [ ] Get a **technique briefing** at the shop: how to clip, never unclipped on the cable, passing other climbers

| Road | Day | Cost (approx.) | Notes |
|------|-----|----------------|-------|
| Großglockner High Alpine Road | 1 | ~€37–40 / car | Day pass. Cash or card at gate. |
| A13 Brenner (Austria) | 4 | ~€10–12 / car | Requires Austrian vignette + Brenner toll |
| A22 (Italy, Brenner → Bressanone exit) | 4 | ~€5–8 / car | Pay at exit booth |
| A23 (Italy, return via Tarvisio) | 5 | ~€5–8 / car | Pay at exit or toll gate |

**Austrian motorway vignette** (A12, A2, A13): required throughout the trip. 10-day vignette ~€9.90 (2024). Buy online at https://www.asfinag.at/en/toll/vignette or at any Austrian petrol station / border.

## Packing list

### Documents
- [ ] ID / passport
- [ ] Driving licence
- [ ] Vehicle registration + insurance (green card recommended for Italy)
- [ ] Austrian vignette (on windscreen)
- [ ] Travel insurance details

### In the car
- [ ] Paper map / atlas as backup (mobile signal in Ötztal and Dolomites can be patchy)
- [ ] Phone mount + charger
- [ ] Sunglasses
- [ ] Reusable water bottles (refillable at Alpine fountains)
- [ ] Small first-aid kit
- [ ] Cash (€) — some mountain toll gates, parking, and huts are cash-only

### Clothing (per person)
- [ ] Waterproof jacket (mandatory — Alpine weather changes fast)
- [ ] Warm layer / fleece (Nordkette ridge, Fuscher Lacke, Cinque Torri — all above 2,000 m)
- [ ] Sturdy hiking shoes / boots (Day 3 ferrata requires ankle support)
- [ ] Trail shoes or comfortable walking shoes (Days 1, 2, 4, 5)
- [ ] Sun hat + sunscreen (high-altitude UV is strong in July–Aug)
- [ ] Gloves (optional — useful on Nordkette ridge and Stuibenfall)
