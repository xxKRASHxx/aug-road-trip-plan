# Day 4 — Ötztal → Timmelsjoch → Jaufenpass → Cortina

**Date:** `[ TBD ]`  
**Drive:** ~3h 25 min (plus pass stops)  
**Distance:** ~216 km  
**Theme:** Two iconic alpine passes through South Tyrol into the Dolomites

This is the **scenic alternative to the Brenner motorway** — slower (about the same door-to-door), but two of the Alps' most spectacular paved pass roads back-to-back.

## Main activities

### 1. Timmelsjoch pass drive (Passo del Rombo) ⭐⭐⭐

- **What:** Austria's most spectacular paved pass road at **2,509 m**, connecting Ötztal with the Passeier valley
- **Toll:** ~€22 / car (2025 prices), purchased at the Austrian toll gate above Hochgurgl
- **Season:** Open roughly **late May through late October** — closes with first heavy snow. **Check the official status before locking this route.**
- **Duration:** ~60–90 min including 20–30 min at the summit architecture pavilions (Matthias Schmuck installations — short walks, excellent views)
- **Views:** Ötztal Alps north, Passeier valley and Dolomites south from the summit
- **Restrictions:** No caravans or trailers
- **Fallback:** If closed, skip to Sterzing via Brenner A13/A22 (conventional Day 4)

### 2. Jaufenpass drive (Passo Giovo) ⭐⭐

- **What:** Free public pass (SS44) at **2,094 m** connecting Passeier and Sterzing with ~20 switchbacks each side
- **Season:** Open year-round barring heavy snow — simpler and less exposed than Timmelsjoch
- **Duration:** ~45–60 min summit-to-summit (Timmelsjoch to Jaufenpass via Walten)
- **Tip:** Coffee / strudel at the small Gasthaus at the summit
- **Note:** Less-touristed than Timmelsjoch; traffic usually light except on weekends

## Significant stops

- **Sölden** (1,368 m) — Ötztal's best-known ski town; last fuel before the pass toll gate
- **Walten / Valtina** (1,300 m) — Passeier-valley hamlet, frazione of St. Leonhard. Gasthaus lunch stop with the Pfarrkirche St. Anton on the roadside
- **Sterzing / Vipiteno** (948 m) — Medieval arcaded town; quick stretch / coffee on Neustadt before the A22 south

## Practical notes

- **Timmelsjoch toll:** ~€22 / car — cash or card at the Austrian gate. One-way price covers the whole pass; the Italian descent is free.
- **A22 toll** (Sterzing → Brixen, Italy): ~€3–5 / car, pay-per-use at toll booths
- **Vignette:** Austria requires the motorway vignette (already needed for the trip), but **Timmelsjoch is NOT part of the motorway network** — the toll is separate
- **Cortina parking:** The town center is partially ZTL (restricted traffic zone) in summer. Use **Fiames** (north of town, free) or **Parcheggio Olimpia** (paid, central). Ask your accommodation.
- **Altitude:** Cortina sits at 1,224 m — noticeably cooler than the valley; bring a layer for the evening
- **Season risk:** If you travel in shoulder season (May, October), check Timmelsjoch status the day before. Jaufenpass is a reliable year-round fallback.
