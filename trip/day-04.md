## Main activity — Lago Ghedina

- **What:** A small, hidden mountain lake ~2 km from central Cortina at 1,536 m elevation
- **Walk:** Easy flat path from the road, ~30 min each way, no elevation gain. Perfect for legs that are tired after the Day 3 ferrata.
- **Why:** Turquoise-green water surrounded by pine forest and the Dolomite rock walls above — beautiful, and far less crowded than the main tourist viewpoints
- **How to get there:** Drive 5 min from Cortina center toward Pocol (SS48b), park at the small roadside lot. Walk the signed trail to the lake.
- **Duration:** ~1–1.5h total including the walk in and out

## Practical notes

- **Brenner A13 toll** (Innsbruck → Brenner): ~€10–12 / car
- **A22 toll** (Brenner → Bressanone exit, Italy): ~€5–8 / car
- **Vignette:** Austria A13 requires the Austrian motorway vignette (already needed for the trip). Italy A22 is pay-per-use at toll booths — cash or card accepted.
- **Cortina parking:** The town center is partially ZTL (restricted traffic zone) in summer. Use the **Fiames parking area** (north of town, free) or **Parcheggio Olimpia** (paid, central). Ask your accommodation for current rules.
- **Altitude:** Cortina sits at 1,224 m — noticeably cooler than the valley; bring a layer for the evening
