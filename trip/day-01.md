## Main activity — Fuscher Lacke

- **What:** Small high-alpine lake at 2,262 m on the Großglockner High Alpine Road
- **Walk:** Easy flat perimeter path, ~20 min loop, no elevation gain
- **Why it's the highlight:** One of the most photographed spots in Austria — the Großglockner (3,798 m) and surrounding glaciers frame the lake. Clear mornings give mirror-like reflections.
- **Crowds:** July–August is busy; arrive at the lake mid-afternoon (post-lunch rush)
- **Footwear:** Trail shoes sufficient; the path is gravel/grassed

## Practical notes

- **Toll road hours:** ~06:00–21:30 (check exact date on grossglockner.at — can vary)
- **July–August tip:** Arrive at the Heiligenblut toll gate **before 13:00** if possible; afternoon queues can be 20–30 min
